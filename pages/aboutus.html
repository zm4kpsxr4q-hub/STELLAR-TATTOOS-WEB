Our Story

About Us

Welcome to Stellar Tattoos, where art meets skin. Our studio is dedicated to providing exceptional tattoo and piercing services, creating unique designs that reflect your individuality. With a team of skilled artists and a commitment to hygiene and safety, we strive to make every visit a memorable experience.

At Stellar Tattoos, we believe in the power of self-expression through body art. Whether you're looking for a small, intricate piece or a full sleeve, our artists work closely with you to bring your vision to life. We also offer piercing services, ensuring a safe and comfortable experience for all clients.

Our studio is located in the heart of Cape Town, South Africa, and we welcome clients from all walks of life. Join us on this artistic journey and let us help you express yourself through the art of tattoos and piercings.

Our Mission

Our mission at Stellar Tattoos is to provide a safe and welcoming environment for clients to express themselves through body art. We are committed to delivering high-quality tattoo and piercing services, while maintaining the highest standards of hygiene and professionalism. Our goal is to create a space where creativity thrives, and where every client leaves with a piece of art that they can proudly wear for a lifetime.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be recognized as a leading tattoo and piercing studio in Cape Town, known for our exceptional artistry, customer service, and commitment to safety. We aim to foster a community of artists and clients who share a passion for self-expression and creativity. Through continuous learning and innovation, we strive to push the boundaries of body art and inspire others to embrace their individuality.
